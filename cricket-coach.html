    How the analysis works

    Your videos never leave your browser as raw video — pose estimation runs fully client-side (MediaPipe) to track 33 body landmarks per sampled frame. From that, the tool detects the five batting phases (stance, backlift, downswing, impact, follow-through) from your hand-speed profile, and computes biomechanical metrics: head stability, weight transfer, backlift straightness, front elbow height, hip-shoulder rotation, and balance. Only a handful of still key-frame images plus the numeric metrics are sent to the coaching model for interpretation — modelled as a combined BCCI Level 2 / ICC Level 3 coach persona. Because this uses a single camera and no bat/ball tracking, treat angle numbers as strong estimates, not lab-grade measurement — the qualitative coaching (what to work on, and how) is the main value.
